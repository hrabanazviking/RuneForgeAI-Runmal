   agree with each other through their public contracts;
   it adds no capability, and Part XI still ends at the
   P-186 gate, which is not this slice.

   files, caps unhit except in the dedicated depth-cap
   test); cap arithmetic at MAX_ENTRIES / MAX_NODES /
   MAX_MATCHES stays pinned by the slices' own batteries,
   which this battery runs beside as neighbours.

   estate's standing law); the battery's pure-read pin
   covers the resolves it runs, not concurrent writers.
