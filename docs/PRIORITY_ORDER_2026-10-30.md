## §1 What the census actually showed, in priority terms

1. **Text-chat depth is a depth problem, not an absence.**
   SillyTavern's 74 rows land 11 HAVE, 45 HAVE-THIN, 18
   MISSING, 0 DELIBERATELY-DIFFERENT (P-004 counts): the cores
   Volmarr uses daily already exist, and the deltas are named
   sub-depths with defined homes in Parts II–VI. Daily use is
   served *first* by deepening what is already in his hands.
2. **Voice is already strong; its gap is breadth and one
   honest performance fact.** The full real-time loop is HAVE
   (VX-09; founding S501–S507), and the voice rows around it
   are mostly HAVE-THIN. What is missing is provider breadth
   (P-010: 10 WANTED-CONNECT, 4 WANTED-GENERIC, 0 HAVE among
   the mirrors' named providers) and the CosyVoice CPU
   performance fact P-006 carried (measured p50 23.15 s /
   RTF 6.73 against unmet GPU-reference thresholds). Voice
   does not need to wait for Part VII to be *usable* — it is
   usable now; Part VII is depth, and the breadth items are
   the small NO-HOME adapters ranked in §3.
3. **Scenarios are the road's one true absence.** The
   scenario block VX-28–VX-47 is 20 rows with no HAVE among
   them: 17 MISSING, 2 HAVE-THIN on substrates only (VX-34
   variables, VX-41 message types), 1 DELIBERATELY-DIFFERENT
   (VX-42, arbitrary-code scripting refused). This is the
   largest single block of MISSING rows in either mirror, and
   it is the territory Volmarr does not have at all today.
4. **The Hub is not catch-up work.** VX-59 is
   DELIBERATELY-DIFFERENT: no central store, by sovereign
   design; its replacement (signed local packs +
   friend-instance exchange, Part XII) is design work on top
   of pack formats that Parts II, III, IV and VI create
   (P-028 archive growth, P-045 book sharing, P-063 prompt
   packs, P-083 plugin packs). Nothing daily waits on it.
5. **The back half of the road is not yet definable, let
   alone movable.** Parts VII–XIV exist in the filed roadmap
   as titles and slice ranges only — the discrepancy recorded
   in `CAMPAIGN_STATE.md` at the opening and confirmed
   material by P-004 (34 of the 55 MISSING rows carry a
   title-only road home). A part with no slice definitions
   cannot be pulled forward in execution order; it can only
   be *prioritised for definition*, which is what §3 does.

## §2 The dependency chain the defined slices already declare

The §3 order is not merely numeric habit; the defined slices
name their own prerequisites, and they point forward in
numeric order:

- Part IX scenarios consume the **variables & state store of
  P-093** (Part VI; its own text: "Part IX scenarios consume
  the same store") and are rebuilt under the **plugin sandbox
  law of P-082** (Part VI) — VX-42's sovereign replacement is
  declared events/actions/variables, not code, and that law
  is Part VI work.
- Part IX's scenario interop is **P-026** (Part II), and the
  typed scenario surface rests on cards and lorebooks as
  Parts II–III deepen them.
- Part X consumes the **image-generation plugin of P-087**
  (Part VI; its own text: "Part X consumes for avatars") and
  the **card assets of P-023** (Part II; "Part X animates
  them").
- Part XII's packs carry what **P-045, P-063 and P-083**
  create (Parts III, IV, VI) inside the archive **P-028**
  grows (Part II).
- Part XIII's Inspector consumes the **lore activation
  inspector of P-043** (Part III) and the **prompt preview of
  P-059** (Part IV), and its scenario half (VX-65) cannot
  exist before Part IX scenarios do.
- Part XIV is the proof spine: the parity matrix *publishes*
  the other parts' results, so it is last by definition, not
  by preference.

Re-sequencing VII–XIII ahead of II–VI would therefore not
bring voice or scenarios sooner; it would build them on
substrates that do not yet exist and have to be built anyway.

## §4 The move that was considered and rejected

**Considered:** renumbering or re-sequencing the road so
Part VII (Voice) and Part IX (Scenarios) execute immediately
after Part I, ahead of Parts II–VI — the literal reading of
"voice + scenarios early".

**Rejected, for three recorded reasons:**

1. **It would not deliver either sooner.** Voice's daily
   surface already exists (§1 point 2); scenarios cannot be
   built before the P-082 sandbox law, the P-093 state store,
   and the Part II interop they consume (§2) — those builds
   would simply happen *inside* VII/IX under other names,
   and the parts they were taken from would be left hollow.
2. **Neither part can be executed yet at all.** VII and IX
   have no slice definitions in the filed roadmap (§1
   point 5). An execution order over undefined slices is a
   fiction; the honest early-move available is the
   definition priority granted in §3 point 3, and it is
   granted.
3. **The census does not ask for it.** P-004's priority note
   — the delta table's one direct word to this slice — says
   the table *supports* the roadmap's stated instinct with
   the order as shaped: voice strong, scenarios absent, hub
   sovereign-late. The instinct is satisfied in substance by
   §3 (voice breadth ranked first at the gate, voice and
   scenarios first to be defined in the back block, hub
   late); a renumbering would be motion, not priority.

What would re-open this question: Volmarr's own word, or the
Evaluation Sitting's findings landing on a Part VII/IX-shaped
gap as Broken — in which case P-006's interrupt rule (§3
point 0) applies first and this record is revisited by a new
entry, never edited in place.
